About the channel

For engineers who want to think like architects.

Each video takes one concept and breaks it down with clear isometric diagrams, from first principles to real-world design, so you understand not just what to build but why.

  • Preparing for system design interviews. Learn the building blocks interviewers expect you to reason about.
  • Shipping with AI tools. Code is cheap now. Knowing how the pieces fit together is the hard part.
  • Growing toward senior and architect roles. Get comfortable with the trade-offs behind real systems.
  • Simply curious. See how the apps you use every day hold up at scale.
